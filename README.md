# Vita Mahjong Guide

# What Is Vita Mahjong?

Vita Mahjong is a free browser-based Mahjong Solitaire game featuring large, easy-to-read tiles and a clean interface. It is especially popular among players who prefer a comfortable visual experience, including seniors and casual puzzle fans.

Unlike traditional four-player Mahjong, Vita Mahjong is a single-player puzzle where the goal is to clear every tile by matching identical free pairs.

# How to Play

The objective is simple:

1. Find two matching free tiles.
2. A tile must have at least one side open.
3. Remove matching pairs.
4. Continue until the board is cleared.

Winning depends on planning rather than speed.

---

# Tips

- Clear upper layers first.
- Open hidden tiles early.
- Look across the whole board before making a move.
- Keep multiple matching options available.
- Use hints only when you get stuck.

---

# Strategy

Experienced players often recommend:

- Plan several moves ahead.
- Avoid removing tiles that block future matches.
- Unlock the center of the layout first.
- Save uncommon tiles until more pairs appear.
- Focus on creating flexibility instead of clearing quickly.

---

# Walkthrough

A typical successful game looks like this:

- Scan the complete board.
- Remove exposed pairs.
- Open blocked layers.
- Continue creating new matching opportunities.
- Finish the remaining tiles carefully.

Most difficult layouts become much easier with patience and observation.

# History

Mahjong Solitaire became popular on personal computers during the 1980s and later evolved into one of the world's favorite casual puzzle games. Modern HTML5 technology now allows players to enjoy games like Vita Mahjong instantly in any browser.
